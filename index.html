Built for real shifts

Everything a rotation needs

No accounts, no servers holding your data — your schedule lives on your device and syncs privately through your own iCloud. Sharing is opt-in, read-only, and revocable any time.

01 · Dashboard

Know your status

A real-time duty ring and countdown badge shows exactly where you stand in your rotation, at a glance, on iPhone and iPad.

02 · Calendar

Full month control

Color-coded month grid with tap-to-override controls for vacation days, extra shifts, and on-call assignments.

03 · Sharing

Share your schedule

Generate a one-time 6-character access code so coworkers and family can see your availability, read-only, without an account.

04 · Recovery

Private by design

Your schedule lives on your device. AES-256 encrypted recovery keys let you back up and restore across devices without an account.

05 · Apple Watch

On your wrist

A companion watchOS app with a progress ring, 7-day shift forecast, and full calendar — right where you need it.

06 · Widgets

Home screen, ready

Small, medium, and large widgets with configurable schedule selection keep your status visible without opening the app.
