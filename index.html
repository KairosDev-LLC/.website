Built for real shifts

Everything a rotation needs

No accounts, no servers holding your data — your schedule lives on your device and syncs privately through your own iCloud. Sharing is opt-in, read-only, and revocable any time.

01 · Dashboard

Know your status

A real-time duty ring and countdown badge shows exactly where you stand in your rotation, at a glance, on iPhone and iPad.

02 · Calendar

Full month control

Color-coded month grid with tap-to-override controls for vacation days, extra shifts, and on-call assignments.

03 · Sharing

Share your schedule

Generate a 6-character access code so coworkers and family can see your availability, read-only, without an account.

04 · Recovery

Private by design

Your schedule lives on your device. AES-256 encrypted recovery keys let you back up and restore across devices without an account.

05 · Apple Watch

On your wrist

A companion watchOS app with a progress ring, 7-day shift forecast, and full calendar — right where you need it.

06 · Widgets

Home screen, ready

Small, medium, and large widgets with configurable schedule selection keep your status visible without opening the app.

Sharing, without accounts

One code. That's the whole setup.

Your family does not sign up for anything. You generate a code, you text it to them, and their copy of Kairos starts showing your live status. Roll a sample code below to see the format.

KAIROS

Six characters, no ambiguous letters or digits.

Step 01

Generate

Create a share code from your schedule. It represents a read-only view — never edit access.

Step 02

Send

Text it, say it out loud, write it on the fridge. Six characters is short enough to pass along verbally.

Step 03

Revoke

Change your mind later and the code stops working. Sharing stays opt-in and reversible.
